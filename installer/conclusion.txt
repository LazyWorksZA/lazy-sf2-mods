The Lazy SF2 Patch is installed into your Strike Fighters 2 folder.

To play, start Strike Fighters 2 as usual, from its Start menu or desktop shortcut.

The new settings are in the game:
- Options > Graphics: Display Mode, Screen
- Options > Controls: Mouse Pointer

If the game was not found, open Lazy SF2 Patch from the Start menu and choose the game folder.

To remove the Lazy SF2 Patch, uninstall it from Windows' Installed apps.
