The Lazy SF2 Patch adds display and mouse options to Strike Fighters 2 (July 2013 patch):

- Display Mode: Fullscreen or Borderless (Options > Graphics)
- Screen: which monitor the game runs on (Options > Graphics)
- Mouse Pointer: match the Windows cursor, or the stock game pointer (Options > Controls)

It also fixes Alt-Tab back into Fullscreen, display scaling above 100%, and the Windows key in Borderless.

Setup copies one file (winmm.dll) into your Strike Fighters 2 folder, and, if Tacview is installed, adds the mod's Tacview add-on for your Windows account and turns it on (close Tacview first). No game files are changed, and uninstalling removes the mod and the add-on again.

The Lazy SF2 Patch is a fan-made mod, not affiliated with Third Wire Productions. You need your own copy of the game.
